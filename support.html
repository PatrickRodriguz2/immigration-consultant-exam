Immigration Consultant Exam · iPhone, iPad & Mac

Support

An independent study app for candidates preparing for the RCIC Entry-to-Practice Exam.

Questions, bug reports, or a question you think is wrong? Email patrguzz@gmail.com. Corrections to the question bank are especially welcome — please include the question and the source you are working from (the Act or regulation and section, the rule, the guideline, or the case).

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 26.0 or later, or a Mac running macOS 26.0 or later. Studying works entirely offline; only buying or restoring Full Access needs a connection. It appears as “Consult Prep” under its icon.

Free to try, and Full Access

The app is free to try: 50 practice questions in any mode, one mock paper (full or half) and every Library card. Full Access is a one-time in-app purchase — no subscription — that unlocks every question, unlimited mock papers and every drill on your iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account.

Restoring a purchase: open the Library tab and tap “Restore purchases” in the Full Access card, or use the same button on the purchase screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why there is no pass mark

The College sets the pass mark for each sitting and does not publish it, and it reports results as pass or fail. So the app shows a projected score out of the 125 scored questions and lets you set your own target (75% by default, adjustable from 60% to 90% in the Library). Your target is yours — it is not the College's pass mark.

How mock papers are built

A full paper has 125 scored questions sampled to the unit shares published in the College's Candidate Guide, about 35 of them in case sets, plus 10 unscored pilot questions placed at random — the same shape as the real exam. The pilot questions are answered like any other and revealed on the result page; they do not count toward your score. A half paper has 63 scored and 5 pilot questions in 90 minutes.

Which law does the app follow?

The law as of September 2026, for the October 2026 and March 2027 sittings. Provisions that were enacted but not yet in force, proposed regulations, and figures that change during the year (fees, income thresholds, draw cut-offs) are deliberately not tested.

Progress and resetting

All progress lives on the device you study on — iPhone, iPad and Mac each keep their own, and nothing syncs between them. To start over, use “Reset progress” in the Library tab. Deleting the app also deletes all progress — there is no cloud copy.

A question looks wrong?

Every question carries an explanation and a reference. If you believe an answer conflicts with the current law, email the question text and your source — verified corrections ship in the next update. This app is exam practice, not advice: always confirm current law and requirements with official sources.
